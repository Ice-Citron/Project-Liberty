The Matrix
The Two Columns
A similarity needs both: perpendicular columns, and equal lengths.
The Similarity Test
Similarity means AᵀA = s²I. Off-diagonal zero, diagonal equal.
Singular values are the axes of the image of the unit circle. Similarity means σ1 = σ2.
Coordinates Ride Along
Both rows agree. The matrix product is the same as x times landed i-hat plus y times landed j-hat.
Linear
Grid lines stay parallel and evenly spaced. The origin stays put. That is all "linear" means.
Columns
The matrix records two landing spots. Column one is where i-hat lands. Column two is where j-hat lands.
Similarity
A similarity lands i-hat and j-hat as a perpendicular pair of equal length. The grid becomes a rotated, resized copy of itself. Squares stay squares.
Generic
Shear and squash are linear too, but they break that pairing. Squares become parallelograms. Angles change.
Determinant
det A is the area factor of the unit square. A negative sign means the orientation flipped, so the F reads backwards.
GigaPose
"In GigaPose, the mapping Mt→q from template coordinates xt to query coordinates xq is constrained to a similarity transform: a rigid rotation, uniform scaling, and translation without shearing." So its linear part has the form s·R(α), and a translation t follows. Chapter 3 takes that formula apart.
