Camera model
Fitted Mt→q · least squares on 10 point pairs
xq = s · R(α) · xt + t. Four numbers: s, α, tx, ty. The fit is the closed-form Procrustes solution. It cannot shear, so any shear shows up as residual.

The Essence
GigaPose splits the 6 DoF of pose. The template fixes the 2 out-of-plane DoF. Mt→q carries the other 4.
Rotate about the optical axis, move closer, slide sideways. The template image only rotates, scales uniformly and shifts. That is Sim(2).
A shear or a non-uniform stretch means the viewpoint moved. That is a different template, not a different M.
4 DoF needs 2 point pairs. With s and α predicted per patch, 1 pair is enough. Fewer samples make RANSAC faster and harder to fool.
Under weak perspective the similarity is exact. Under full perspective a small residual remains. The refinement stage removes it.
"In GigaPose, the mapping Mt→q from template coordinates xt to query coordinates xq is constrained to a similarity transform: a rigid rotation, uniform scaling, and translation without shearing."
