The Essence
Similar matrices is a relation between two matrices. A' = B-1AB says: same map, new coordinates. The picture does not change. The description does.
A similarity transform is one map on points. It rotates, scales uniformly and shifts. The picture changes. Its shape does not.
B-1AB is usually not a similarity transform. Change the basis on the left and watch B-1AB shear while A itself stays put.
The two meanings meet in one case. Conjugate by a similarity S = sR: S A S-1 = R A R-1. That is A written in a rotated frame, and it is similar to A.
In GigaPose, Mt→q is the second meaning. It maps template pixels to query pixels with a rigid rotation, a uniform scale and a translation, and no shear.
