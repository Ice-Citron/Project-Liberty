    The Essence
    Rotation ⊂ Rigid ⊂ Similarity ⊂ Affine ⊂ Projective. Each step outward adds freedom and drops an invariant. Read the checklist top to bottom and you read the hierarchy.
    Similarity is the last family in which a circle stays a circle and angles survive. Shapes keep their shape. Only size, heading and position change.
    Affine keeps parallel lines and one area factor, but bends squares into parallelograms. Projective keeps only straightness, so parallel lines may meet.
    More degrees of freedom means more correspondences per hypothesis and more ways for RANSAC to fit noise. A tighter family is a stronger prior.
    "In GigaPose, the mapping Mt→q from template coordinates xt to query coordinates xq is constrained to a similarity transform: a rigid rotation, uniform scaling, and translation without shearing." A shear or an anisotropic stretch in the image would mean the viewpoint changed. That change belongs to the choice of template, not to Mt→q.
    Names
    SO(2) rotations. SE(2) rigid motions. Sim(2) similarities. Aff(2) affine maps. PGL(3) projective maps of the plane, also called homographies.
