Essence of Similarity Transforms

Five short chapters. Each one asks a visual question first, lets you drag the picture, and only then writes the formula. The goal: check your mental picture of linear transforms, and see exactly what makes a similarity different from a generic one.

In GigaPose, the mapping Mt→q from template coordinates xt to query coordinates xq is constrained to a similarity transform: a rigid rotation, uniform scaling, and translation without shearing. The sentence every chapter comes back to.

Chapters

Chapter 1 · about 10 min A Transform Is Where the Grid Lands Where do i-hat and j-hat land? That is the whole matrix. Drag them and watch the grid follow. a similarity lands them as a perpendicular, equal-length pair, so squares stay squares. Chapter 2 · about 10 min The Transform Hierarchy Rotation ⊂ Rigid ⊂ Similarity ⊂ Affine ⊂ Projective. Each step adds freedom and loses an invariant. similarity is the last family in which a circle stays a circle. Chapter 3 · about 10 min Anatomy of a Similarity xq = s·R(α)·xt + t. Four numbers. Add shear or a squash and see the test fail. ATA = s2I, or equally, both singular values are equal. Chapter 4 · about 15 min · the answer Why GigaPose Uses a Similarity A pinhole camera, a template pose and a query pose. Move the object in-plane, in depth, sideways. Then tilt it. the template fixes the viewpoint; the leftover 4 DoF are exactly a similarity. Chapter 5 · about 8 min Two Meanings of "Similar" B-1AB versus s·R·x + t. Same word, different objects. Do not mix them up in your notes. similar matrices change the description; a similarity transform changes the picture.
